FAQ

Good to know

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes. Reserving ahead is a good idea for big groups, especially on weekend nights.

How late are you open?

We are open every day from 7 AM to 2 AM. The kitchen takes the last order at 1 AM.

Can I hold a small event or meeting here?

Yes. Choose “9+ guests (event)” in the reservation form or send us a message about events and bulk orders.

Do you have promos?

Yes. Rice bowls are only ₱169 (instead of ₱250) from 9–11 AM and 2–4 PM. Join our Facebook community for new promos.

Why do the BBQ ribs have a price range?

Our Texas Style BBQ Pork Ribs are priced by weight: Solo (200–250 g), Half Slab (300–500 g) or Full Slab (550–1000 g).
