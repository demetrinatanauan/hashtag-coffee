Our story

Why “Hashtag”?

Every great cup comes with a moment people want to share – a finished thesis, a reunion, a first date, a Sunday with family. The hashtag is our way of saying: this place is for those moments.

The Capitol Site branch sits along the National Highway in Kumintang Ibaba, right in front of the Batangas Provincial Capitol and close to schools, offices and banks. It opens at 7 AM and stays open until 2 AM, so it works for a quick morning coffee, a filling lunch, or a late night with friends.

  • Coffee, matcha, milk tea, frappes and full meals in one place
  • Open late – until 2 AM, every day
  • Platters and party trays for groups and small celebrations
